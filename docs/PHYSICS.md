# Physics

## Ultrasonic distance
`L = t·c/2 − offset`, `c(ϑ) = 331.3 m/s + 0.606 m/(s·°C)·ϑ` (≈ 343.4 m/s at 20 °C). The median of 50 pings suppresses outliers.

## Diffraction at a thin fibre
By Babinet's principle a fibre of diameter d produces (away from the direct beam) the same Fraunhofer pattern as a slit of width d:

`I(θ) ∝ (sin β / β)²,  β = π d sin θ / λ`, minima at `d sin θ = m λ`.

With the minima spacing a (left ↔ right, order m) on a screen at distance L:
`tan θ = (a/2)/L`, `d = m λ / sin θ`.

Example (spider silk, run A04, sample 1): L = 39.88 cm, a = 14.2 cm, m = 2 →
θ = 10.1°, d = 2·650 nm / sin 10.1° = **7.42 µm**.

## Tensile test
Force per fibre = F<sub>max</sub> / n after zero-offset correction (median of the first 20 samples). Fibre diameters from the diffraction station allow converting forces into stresses (σ = F / (π d²/4)).
